Privacy & settings

What gets sent

ScamLens reads a page only when you press Analyze or use the right-click menu. The page address, title, readable text and up to 60 links (or the text you selected or pasted) are sent over HTTPS to the analysis service to produce the report. Nothing is scanned in the background. If you sign in with Google, your account ID is sent with each scan so the service can identify you. Scan history is kept in your ScamLens account when you're signed in, otherwise only on this device; you can delete it any time from History.
